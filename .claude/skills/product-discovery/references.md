# Product practice — sources and extra templates

Research behind the `product-discovery`, `product-strategy` and `product-launch` skills (web
research 2026-10-20). Read when you need the original framing; the skills hold the
CityTurfer-specific rules.

## Which frameworks, and why (ranked for a solo, pre-PMF, analytics-free consumer app)

1. Customer interviews — Mom Test + JTBD switch (interviews _are_ the data system)
2. Positioning against alternatives — Dunford (crowded niche)
3. PMF measurement — Ellis / Vohra, small-n
4. Assumption mapping + cheap tests — Bland, Savoia, Torres
5. Opportunity Solution Tree / outcome framing — Torres, Cagan
6. Appetite-based scoping — Shape Up
7. Strategy kernel + DHM — Rumelt, Biddle
8. Privacy-preserving measurement — HEART-lite
9. Ethical engagement — regret test
10. One-page specs / PR-FAQ
11. Now / Next / Later roadmap
12. Launch for the first 100 users
13. Pricing discovery — Van Westendorp, Ramanujam

**Overkill for now**: numeric RICE reach, North Star metric trees and dashboards, AARRR funnels, A/B
tests (n too small), OKR cascades, Kano and ODI surveys (need n ≥ 30 per segment), event taxonomies,
product ops.

## Anti-patterns to refuse

Human: feature factory (output over outcome), founder-as-only-user, solution-first questions ("would
you use…?"), no pre-registered criterion, premature scaling (monetisation, social, polish before
retention), dates on Later, vanity metrics (waitlist size without activation), dark-pattern
engagement, "just one analytics SDK", copycat parity.

AI-as-PM: fabricated evidence or personas; false precision; sycophantic agreement (argue the
strongest case against every bet — a pre-mortem); artifact theatre; confusing ticket-writing with
product work; privacy shortcuts in measurement; trusting competitor SEO pages; contacting users or
impersonating the founder; stale facts without dates.

## PR-FAQ (Working Backwards, short)

```text
# <Headline a Turfer would repeat>
<City>, <date> — one-sentence summary. Problem paragraph in the customer's words. Solution paragraph.
Founder quote. Customer quote (HYPOTHETICAL — label it). How to start.
External FAQ: Is my location uploaded? How is this different from Strava / CityStrides / Squadrats?
  Battery? Price?
Internal FAQ: Riskiest assumption? What would make us kill it? Why now? What do we not build?
```

## Opportunity Solution Tree (markdown form)

```text
Outcome: <one measurable-ish outcome, e.g. testers recording ≥3 activities in weeks 2–4>
- O1 <need/pain/desire heard>                [evidence: E-003, E-007]
  - S1a <solution>  - S1b <solution>  - S1c <solution>
    - assumption (type) → test → pre-registered criterion
```

## Weekly cadence

Mon: evidence log + re-rank assumptions · Wed: refresh one pitch or pick the next test · Fri:
"learned / changed / next" note, flag any decision taken without evidence · Per version: PMF survey
(once there are qualified users), betting memo (bets taken and not taken), positioning canvas
review.
