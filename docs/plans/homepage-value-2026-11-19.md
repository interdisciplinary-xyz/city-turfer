# Homepage that explains the value

🚧 **Proposed on 2026-11-19 by `product-manager` for branch `feat/web-value-homepage` (cut from
`scope/v0.17.0`).** Source of truth for what the app does:
[feature scope](../product/feature-scope.md) (re-checked against code on 2026-11-19). Code wins over
this plan.

**This supersedes the founder call of 2026-11-12 (#229, commit `92dfc53`)** that cut the homepage to
the header and the hero. The founder now asks for a page that explains in detail what value we
deliver. Most of the pieces already exist and are unmounted; this plan reuses them, corrects the
claims that became false, and adds what shipped since (Districts, Daily goal, Share photo). It also
fixes #227 must-fix item 1 (both hero buttons are dead links) and feature-scope decision 6.

Earlier homepage plans: `docs/plans/homepage-sections-2026-10-28.md` and
`docs/plans/homepage-tidy-2026-11-01.md` (both executed; this plan replaces their section list).

## 0. Outcome and how we'll know

**Outcome.** A city walker, runner or rider who lands on the page understands within a minute what
they get (their streets become turf that glows brighter on return, kept on their phone), how far
along the app is, and leaves an email to test it.

**How we'll know, without analytics:**

- Waitlist sign-ups per week from Brevo's own counts, compared with the weeks before the page went
  live (time windows, no trackers).
- Pre-registered hallway check (add to the discovery board before launch of the page): 5 people from
  beachhead 1, not friends, read the page on their phone for 60 seconds and say back what the app
  does. Success: **≥4/5** mention streets or turf that brighten when you return, and **≥3/5**
  mention that routes stay on the phone. This also gives the first evidence for A2 and A5.

**Not doing:** headline variants (Next, A5), fake doors (Later), a privacy policy page (v0.17.0 item
3, its own work), any live data from the API, a web turf map.

## 1. Who and what job

**Who** (hypothesis, [ICP](../product/icp.md), beachhead 1): a Polish city dweller, 25–45, with an
iPhone and often an Apple Watch, who walks, runs or rides several times a week, mostly on the same
streets. _Assumption_: A6 (walkers are the best fit) is untested.

**Job** (hypothesis, feature scope §1): _"When I head out for my usual walk or run, I want to see my
city fill in and my regular streets glow, so everyday movement feels like it adds up to something."_

**Value promise, one line** (intent; brand-designer words it): _Every street you walk, run or ride
becomes yours for good, and glows brighter each time you come back — kept on your phone._

## 2. Honesty rules for every line on the page

1. **Only Built (or In v0.17.0) features are claimed.** Anything else appears only in the _Not yet_
   list, with no date. Each claim maps to a value-map row (the copy deck's "Value" column).
2. **Thin evidence is labelled.** A feature with no device run on a current build either carries a
   tag (_New_, _iPhone only_) and also appears under _Being tested_, or is not headlined (V3 locked
   screen, V6 Share photo, saving to Apple Health, battery on long outings, backup).
3. **The stage, said plainly, once, near the call to action:** an early test; iPhone first; Android
   is a preview build that has only run on an emulator; App Store and TestFlight not yet.
4. **Account required today.** The privacy section and the FAQ say you sign in with your email for
   now. "No account" and "nothing leaves your phone" are **not** said until v0.17.0 item 1 ships.
   What does leave the phone (map tiles, update checks, gap ends unless turned off, the sign-in
   email) is listed, as today.
5. **No fake numbers.** No user counts, ratings, testimonials or "join 1,000 Turfers". Example
   numbers on the demo and the phone mock-ups come from the Gdańsk demo profile and say so
   (`home.demo.caption`). Counts the code can derive (achievements) come from code, not copy.
6. **Brand vocabulary and ethics:** turf, cell, ride, Your city; never territory, score, points,
   conquer. No urgency ("spots are limited"), no guilt, no leaderboards (brand rule "glow, never
   casino").
7. **Polish is a real translation** (`mobile-i18n` glossary), same keys in `en.json` and `pl.json`.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

1. **Hero buttons:** on `/en` and `/pl`, clicking the primary button lands on `section#join` with
   its heading below the sticky header; the secondary on `section#how`. Checked in Playwright on the
   dummy-env build, and on a build **without** waitlist env (`#join` exists, shows the fallback
   line, no `<form>`, the primary button reads `hero.getIt`).
2. **Claims:** every new or changed key in the copy deck names a value-map row (V1–V10) or is UI
   text; no key claims a feature outside Built / In v0.17.0 except in `home.status.notYet`. Reviewed
   by `product-manager` against this plan before merge. Specific checks: no "past workouts",
   "history" or "day one" for Apple Health; no "no streaks"; no "no account"; no "records with the
   screen off" as a highlight.
3. **Languages:** `pnpm run audit:i18n` passes (same keys, valid ICU, full Polish plurals).
4. **Accessibility:** axe (wcag2a/aa, wcag21a/aa) finds no serious or critical violation on `/en`
   and `/pl` at 320, 390, 768 px; one `h1`; every section `aria-labelledby` its `h2`.
5. **Layout:** no horizontal scroll from 320 to 1440 px (`apps/web/tools/viewports.mjs`); the Polish
   header links fit one row at 1024 px; tiles are 3 + 3 at 768 and 1440 px.
6. **Reduced motion:** with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, every `[data-reveal]` and
   `[data-anim]` element is visible on load; nothing waits for scroll.
7. **Privacy of the site:** the landing spec's checks pass (no cookie, no `/api` request, same
   origin only); `apps/web/out` has no third-party script `src` and GSAP only in the hero chunk.
8. **OSM credit** visible in the hero corner, the demo caption and the footer.
9. **Header:** the scroll-spy marks the section being read; at the page bottom it marks "Join".
10. **Stories:** every changed or new homepage, layout and waitlist component has a story that
    renders in `pnpm --filter @cityturfer/storybook build`.
11. `pnpm run ci:quality` passes.

## 6. Risks and pre-mortem

- **Value:** the page explains well and still nobody signs up, because there is no form live (env
  unset) or because "sign in with your email" puts off the privacy-minded (A5). Mitigation: open
  question 1; the honest account line stays — it is true, and no-account is the v0.17.0 core.
- **Usability:** too long; the glow gets lost in tiles. Mitigation: six tiles, not more; the demo
  stays in the second section; the hallway check in §0.
- **Feasibility:** small; components exist. The risk is test churn in `landing.spec.ts`.
- **Viability and trust:** the page drifts behind the app (the false Apple Health line is exactly
  that). Mitigation: the value-map column in the copy deck and the docs-engineer check.
- **Pre-mortem:** "We launched the page, wrote 'Share photo' and 'Apple Watch counts', a tester
  tried both on day one and neither worked on their iPhone." That is why both carry tags and appear
  under _Being tested_ until the v0.17.0 device run (feature scope §4 item 8).

## 7. Open founder questions

1. **Waitlist env on Vercel:** is it set? Recommendation: set it before this page ships; without it
   the page explains but cannot recruit (feature scope decision 6).
2. **One form, not two.** The 2026-11-01 tidy kept a form under the hero _and_ at the end.
   Recommendation: one form in `#join`, reached by the hero button. Trade-off: one more tap from the
   first screen.
3. **Android APK download on the page?** Recommendation: no, keep "Android: not yet tested on a real
   phone" in _Not yet_ (decision 7: preview until A3 passes). Trade-off: Android visitors (about
   two-thirds of Polish phones) get nothing to try.
4. **Daily goal copy in the app.** "Meet today's goal to keep it going" (`goals.json`, `streakKeep`)
   leans on streak pressure. The page will not say "no streaks". Recommendation: ask
   `brand-designer` for a calmer line in the app. Trade-off: none on the page.
5. **Ship in v0.17.0?** Recommendation: yes; it closes #227 item 1 and decision 6, about 2–3 days of
   the 2-week appetite.
