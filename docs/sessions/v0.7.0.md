# Session log — v0.7.0 (2026-10-19 → 2026-10-20)

v0.7.0 adds **the city picker**. A Turfer chooses their **Home City** (UI: "Your city"), and the
maps centre on it. It is JS plus an additive SQLite migration, with no native change. The work was
done in a parallel session; this log is assembled from its PRs (#84, #85) and the git history.
Design record: `docs/decisions/home-city-2026-10-19.md`.

## Built

**#84 `feat/territory-home-city`** (merged into `scope/v0.7.0` 2026-10-19 12:28 UTC)

- **Choose a city:**
  - Me menu → **City**, the **Record tab's chip at the top** ("⌂ Kraków ▾", hidden while recording),
    or the Turf Map chip.
  - All three open one picker (`apps/mobile/src/app/city.tsx` → `territory/ui/city-picker.tsx`).
  - Search ignores accents ("krakow" → Kraków, "lodz" → Łódź). The current city is ticked; "Clear
    <city>" removes it.
- **Catalogue, no geocoding:** 23 Polish cities (biggest first) and 15 major European cities,
  bundled in `packages/core/src/contexts/territory/domain/city-catalog.ts` (since v0.8.0). It works
  offline, and the choice never leaves the phone.
- **Maps:**
  - **Turf Map** opens on the Home City: its centre ± its radius, so the whole city fits any screen.
    - It flies to a newly chosen city the next time the tab is on screen.
    - The "⌂ Kraków" chip recentres. A long press, or the screen-reader action, changes the city.
    - Without a Home City it behaves as before (fit all tracks, or follow the Turfer) and offers "⌂
      Your city".
  - **Record map** starts on the Home City until the first GPS fix. Before, it showed MapLibre's
    default at 0,0, in the open sea. A city chosen from its chip is flown to; "◎ Recenter" returns
    to the Turfer.
  - Activity detail is unchanged.
- **Model (Territory context):**
  - `GetHomeCity`, `ChooseHomeCity` and `SearchCities` on the Territory API.
  - Domain rule: only catalogue cities.
  - Stored per user in `ter_home_city` (**migration 7**) as the catalogue id only. A
    `HomeCityChosen` event (city id, no coordinates) is saved in the same transaction.
  - Recording never imports Territory: the Record screen hands the city in as a prop.
- **Commits:**
  - `0920814` home city model
  - `4830b6e` picker and Turf Map
  - `1a95c48` docs
  - `e9dd6fa` review fixes
  - `a382d87` Record tab chip
- **Review fixes (mobile-reviewer, `e9dd6fa`):**
  - On iOS, MapLibre ignores a Camera mounted after the map's first layout, so the Turf and Record
    screens load the Home City before mounting their map.
  - Screens refresh right after a choice (not via the outbox).
  - A failed read falls back to "no city".
  - The city chip gets an accessibility action; the picker handles the keyboard.

## Risk noted at release

- **Users:** a "City" item in the Me menu, chips on the Record and Turf tabs, and maps opening on
  the chosen city. Nothing else changes until a city is chosen.
- **Migration 7 is additive** (one new single-row table). A rollback leaves an unused table.
- **The release commit bumps `app.json` 0.6.0 → 0.7.0**, which changes the fingerprint. The iPhone's
  build has the pre-release fingerprint (`9034249b…`), so OTAs from `main` won't reach it. Reinstall
  the iPhone and build a new Android APK from `main`, and publish no OTA before that.
