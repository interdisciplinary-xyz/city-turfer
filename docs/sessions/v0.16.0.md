### Share photo (#225, fixes in #231)

- Plan `docs/plans/share-photo-2026-11-11.md` (founder request 2026-11-11). From an activity (just
  finished, or from History) take or pick a photo and share a 9:16 card of the activity's stats over
  it through the system share sheet, or save it to Photos.
- The card shows distance, moving time, pace or speed, optionally "+N new turf cells", and a place:
  off by default, per card the city or "district, city". **No map, route or coordinates**; the card
  is a fresh render, so the photo's EXIF and GPS never reach the shared JPEG.
- After Finish, a "Share a photo?" card with Not now and Don't ask again; never from History.
- Nothing is written to SQLite, the backup queue or the API. New: the context
  `apps/mobile/src/contexts/share-photo`, the screen `apps/mobile/src/app/share-photo/[id].tsx`,
  Territory's `GetActivityPlace`, `ShareOverlay` in `@cityturfer/ui`, the `share` namespace in en
  and pl, Maestro flows 20 and 21.
- **Native:** expo-image-picker, react-native-view-shot, expo-sharing and expo-file-system, and
  `app.json` changes (camera and photo-save usage strings, blocked storage permissions).
- #231: Take photo works on Android 7–9 (storage permission unblocked); Android share cards stay in
  internal cache and are swept after an hour; flow 20 seeds its own photo; the plan matches the app
  (permission prompts stay English until decision L10); docs count 21 flows.
