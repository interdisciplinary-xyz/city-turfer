## Decisions

- **iOS distribution:** no paid Apple Developer Program for now ($99/year), so no TestFlight or
  ad-hoc builds. iOS testers are limited to the founder's iPhone (Xcode reinstall with a free Apple
  ID, then OTA). The 7-day test week runs on Android.
- **Android distribution:** sideloaded APK, no Play Store. Built in the cloud with **EAS Build**,
  hosted on **Vercel Blob**, linked from the landing page. Testers: friends' phones only.
- **Waitlist:** **Brevo** with double opt-in; the controller is the founder as an individual. The
  landing page stays static: the browser posts straight to Brevo, no server or cookies.
- **Motion:** GSAP + `@gsap/react` on the landing page, with a `web-motion` skill and a
  `web-engineer` agent.
- **Apple Health is the v0.4.0 focus** (founder, 2026-10-18):
  - both directions: import, and save recordings automatically (switch on by default)
  - full history on the first import
  - library `@kingstinct/react-native-healthkit`, chosen over our own Swift module
  - plan first, then execution
  - design: `docs/decisions/apple-health-2026-10-18.md`
- **Homepage:** two download buttons, Android (active) and App Store (disabled, "Coming soon"). On
  desktop the text and phones share one row and the buttons get their own row underneath.
- **Storybook (asked, not started):** a shared design system for web and mobile is possible. The
  recommendation is one web Storybook rendering the mobile components through `react-native-web`,
  next to the web components. Planned as its own scope after Apple Health.

**#67 `feat/apple-health`** (open)

- **New context: Health Sync** (`apps/mobile/src/contexts/health-sync/`), the translation layer
  between Apple Health and our activities.
  - **Import:** walks, hikes, runs and rides with routes become activities (`hk-<UUID>`) that claim
    turf. 25 workouts per page, with the position saved after each page.
  - **Export:** recordings are saved to Health with their route, in the foreground only.
- **Safeguards:**
  - `hs_links` remembers every workout, so nothing is imported or exported twice, deleted imports
    stay deleted, and our own workouts are skipped (metadata key).
  - Late routes (Apple Watch still syncing) are retried for 3 days.
  - Outings already in CityTurfer are skipped (time ranges overlap ≥ 50%).
  - Before saving, the export checks Health for an earlier save in case the app died halfway.
  - Migration 6 adds `hs_state`, `hs_links`, `hs_pending_imports` and `hs_pending_exports`.
- **UI:**
  - Apple Health card on Info: connect, two switches, last import, Import now (a full scan),
    Disconnect.
  - "From Apple Health" on imported activities.
  - Simulator seed tool.
- **Native:**
  - `@kingstinct/react-native-healthkit` 16.2.0 + Nitro modules.
  - Background delivery off.
  - Excluded from Android (`react-native.config.js`).
  - New runtime fingerprints: iOS `e800f53d…`, Android `3dc5b043…` (on the branch, before merging
    with #62).
- **Claude Code:** `apple-health` skill, `health-engineer` agent; a `mobile-reviewer` pass.
- **Problems found:**
  - iOS 26's permission sheet adds a "how much history" step. The card now tells people to pick All
    Recorded Data.
  - Skipping our own workouts by bundle ID also skipped the seed tool's samples; it now uses our
    metadata key.
  - The review found four defects, all fixed with tests:
    - routes that arrived late were skipped for good
    - the same outing from two sources was imported twice
    - a kill between saving the workout and its link could write a second workout
    - the export could run headless
  - The new card pushed the seed button below the fold in flow 06, which now scrolls.
  - Flow 11 reruns produce overlapping samples, so it also accepts "already in CityTurfer".
- **Verified:**
  - 309 Jest tests and `ci:quality`
  - iOS Simulator, Maestro 11/11
  - import with route, turf and label checked on the Simulator
  - export with route checked in the app database, and the re-import skipped our own workout
- **Not yet:** Apple Watch data on the iPhone; an Android prebuild with the exclusion.
