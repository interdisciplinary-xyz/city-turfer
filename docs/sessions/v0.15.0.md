### Current city card and district share (#209)

- **Current city** card on the Dashboard, after This week (`territory/ui/current-city-card.tsx`,
  composed in `apps/mobile/src/app/(tabs)/index.tsx`): the Home City's name; "N of M districts" that
  hold turf, with a bar; the turf in the city and the district with the most; the three districts
  with the most turf, each with a bar and its share. A tap opens Districts.
- Other states: no Home City → "Choose your city" opens the picker; a city without district data
  says so; while the districts load, or after a failed read, the card is not shown. The card reads
  the districts with the same `useDistricts` hook as the Districts screen (chunked, cancellable),
  again after every data change.
- **Districts rows**: a line under the name with a bar in the turf colour and the district's turf
  share as a whole percent (`territory/ui/district-share.ts`): "0%" without turf, "<1%" for a
  sliver, never "0%" with turf. VoiceOver reads it in the row label (en ", 9% of the district", pl
  ", 9% dzielnicy"); the fixed row height counts the new line, so a map tap still scrolls to the
  right row.
- Copy: `turf.json` › `cityCard` in en and pl; en title "Current city", pl „Bieżące miasto” (open:
  „Twoje miasto”, below). The brand-designer read of the new keys is still to do.
- **Open from the #208 review (should-fix, `mobile-ui-engineer`):** the card is one accessible
  element whose label (`cityCard.label`) reads only the city, the turf and "N of M districts";
  VoiceOver never hears the leading district or the top three and their shares.
- Maestro flow 18 also checks the first row's share and the Dashboard card: passed on the iPhone 17
  Pro Simulator, iOS 26.1, per #209.

### Daily goal (#217)

- Founder request (2026-11-10), plan `docs/plans/daily-goal-2026-11-10.md`: an opt-in **Daily goal**
  of 10–180 minutes (steps of 5, 30 suggested) of moving time from walks, runs and rides, Apple
  Health imports included. An activity counts on the local day it started; a live recording counts
  toward the day it started, even past midnight.
- **Dashboard card**: "18 of 30 min", a bar and the last 7 days with ✓ on met days; before a goal is
  set, a hideable "Set a daily goal" offer. **Streak**: a flame and "N-day streak", days in a row
  with the goal met, each day judged by the goal in force that day; today joins once met.
- **Editor**: the `/goal` modal, from the card or Me › Daily goal; a change applies from today, past
  days keep their goal; Turn off is in the editor.
- New code: the Goals context (`apps/mobile/src/contexts/goals`), core query
  `activityLog.startsBetween`, `@cityturfer/ui` `ProgressBar` and `DayStrip` (with stories),
  `LiveSnapshot.startedAt`. Copy en and pl with all Polish plural forms; no "missed", "behind" or
  "lost".
- **Migrations**: 15 adds `goal_daily_targets` and `goal_state` (no coordinates, not backed up). 14
  adds `hs_state.show_history` (below, under Apple Health).
- Maestro flow `19-daily-goal` (new) passed on the iPhone 17 Pro Simulator per #217 (no-streak state
  only; the streak state is covered in Jest). Not seen: minutes rising during a real recording, the
  midnight rollover, VoiceOver, Android.
- Streaks were added against the product and brand advice (founder decision, #217).

### Apple Health import from the moment of connecting (#214)

- Founder decision 2026-11-05: the import (and "Import now") takes only workouts that **end after
  Apple Health was connected** (`hs_state.connected_at`); one still going on at that moment is
  imported, older ones stay in Apple Health. Recorded in `docs/decisions/apple-health-2026-10-18.md`
  (the "Full history" rule struck through and replaced), the runbook, features and the product
  brief.
- The last-import line counts them: "3 workouts added · 27 from before you connected, left in Apple
  Health" (en, pl with all plural forms). The review fixes (`5597775`) keep workouts already linked
  out of that count and keep the last report when a sync finds nothing new.
- The card tip "choose All Recorded Data" is gone. The dev seeder writes sample workouts that end
  now, so flow 11 still imports something.
- Not done: workouts imported before this change stay (no clean-up); disconnecting and reconnecting
  moves the start date, so workouts done while disconnected are never imported.
- **Migration 14** (`ALTER TABLE hs_state ADD COLUMN show_history …`, landed with #217 so the
  numbers stay in order) is for a "show older workouts" switch that is **not on scope**: no code
  reads the column, and its SQL comment ("older imports are removed from CityTurfer") describes
  behaviour that isn't built. Once v0.15.0 ships, v14's SQL can't change.
