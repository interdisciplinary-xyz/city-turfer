# Session log — v0.1.0 (2026-10-15 → 2026-10-16)

From an empty folder to **CityTurfer v0.1.0**: a working iOS proof of concept on a real iPhone, with
over-the-air updates, a GitHub repository, quality gates and an end-to-end regression suite.

## Product

- **CityTurfer** — _Surf your city. Own your streets._ (formerly TurfTrack). Record walks, runs and
  rides; every street you cover becomes your **turf** on an H3 hexagon grid (~50 m cells),
  permanent, glowing brighter with repeat visits.
- Decisions: single user, data on device; mock sign-in `admin@turftrack.com` / `admin1234`; turf is
  permanent (no decay); Turf map always dark; corridor turf (no closed-loop areas); MapLibre +
  OpenFreeMap (no Google key); iOS first (Android SDK not installed on this Mac).

## Day 2 — background tracking fixed (2026-10-16)

### Root cause

Real-device rides kept bursts of about 50 s after each screen lock, then nothing until unlock (walk:
42 min → 0.53 km). The iPhone system log showed RunningBoard **suspending the app while locationd
still held its location assertion**: expo-location's legacy `CLLocationManager` subscription doesn't
keep the app running. A `CLBackgroundActivitySession` on its own (first attempt) didn't help either.

### Built

- **Recording (native)**: `apps/mobile/modules/background-session`: `CLBackgroundActivitySession` +
  `CLLocationUpdate.liveUpdates(.fitness)` (iOS 17+), restart after relaunch, buffers fixes until JS
  listens; expo-location kept as a safety net, one serialized ingest queue, duplicates dropped by
  the Fix Filter
- **Data repair**: Gaps > 45 s split the track (`splitAtGaps`, `Activity.correctTrack`, one-time job
  `split-track-gaps-v1`); turf reclaimed from corrected tracks
- **Gap estimates**: Gaps routed along streets by the public OSM router (only the gap endpoints are
  sent); drawn dashed; distance counted and marked "≈"; estimated parts claim no turf
- **Diagnostics**: `sys_diagnostics` timeline without coordinates;
  `apps/mobile/tools/diagnose-recording.mjs`, `apps/mobile/tools/device-diagnose.sh`,
  `apps/mobile/tools/turf-report.mjs`
- **App shell**: Tab bar Turf · History · raised **Record** · Stats · Me (SF Symbols); new **Stats**
  tab: this week, 8-week chart, turf, all-time per type
- **Code style**: All files kebab-case (46 renamed); shared constants in
  `apps/mobile/src/constants/` (a leaf any layer may import, enforced by dependency-cruiser)

### Verified

- **Real device, 65 min dog walk with the screen locked: 4.07 km**, fixes every minute tagged
  `live:background+session`. The only gaps were auto-pauses while standing still, plus GPS at 46–115
  m near buildings, rejected as it should be.
- `npm run ci:quality`: **31 Jest tests**, dependency rules clean.
- Tab bar and Stats checked on the iOS simulator; OTA `01a144ce` published (runtime `271b7981…`, no
  reinstall).
- Turf audit of earlier rides: claims come only from recorded stretches, about 30–40 cells per km.
  Gaps had hidden about 74 % of those routes (5.7 km recorded vs 15.9 km estimated).
