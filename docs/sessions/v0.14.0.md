### Districts (#195)

- New modal **Districts**, opened from a `turf-districts` chip on the Turf tab, shown only when the
  Home City has district data. Map of the city's OpenStreetMap districts with the turf on top,
  shaded by turf; list of districts with km² of turf and the district's own area, most turf first.
  Tap a row or a district on the map to select it (the map fits, the list scrolls); tap again for
  the whole city. States: no Home City, no data, no turf yet, read failure. Copy in en and pl.
- Data: boundaries for 16 cities (Warsaw, Kraków, Łódź, Wrocław, Poznań, Gdańsk, Szczecin,
  Bydgoszcz, Lublin, Białystok, Katowice, Gdynia, Częstochowa, Rzeszów, Opole, Berlin's 12 Bezirke),
  generated once by `apps/mobile/tools/gen-districts.mjs`, committed in
  `packages/core/src/districts/data/` and exported as `@cityturfer/core/districts` (about 477 KB per
  the PR, loaded lazily per city). The generator refuses a city whose districts cover less than 97 %
  or more than 103 % of it: Toruń (42 %) and Olsztyn (67 %) are out until OSM is complete; Kielce
  and Sopot have no usable district level. Neighbours share simplified borders exactly; areas use
  the same spherical formula as the H3 cells.
- Coverage is a first measure: km² of turf cells whose centre lies in the district. No "lit" or
  "owned" state yet. The ODbL credit sits under the map.
- New Maestro flow `apps/mobile/e2e/18-districts.yaml` (passed twice on the iPhone 17 Pro Simulator,
  iOS 26.1, demo account, per the PR). Plan: `docs/plans/districts-2026-10-29.md`.
- JS only (no `app.json`, plugin, module or dependency change); about 477 KB more JSON in the
  bundle.
