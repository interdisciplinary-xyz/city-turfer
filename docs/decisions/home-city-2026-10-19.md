# Home City (v0.7.0)

Asked for on 2026-10-19: "create a city picker inside the app; the selected city is the target for
map centering". Code wins on conflict.

## Decision

- **Ubiquitous language:** the chosen city is the **Home City** (UI copy: "Your city"). It belongs
  to the **Territory** context: turf lives in a city, and the Turf Map is Territory's.
- **Catalogue, not geocoding.** Cities come from a list bundled with the app
  (`packages/core/src/contexts/territory/domain/city-catalog.ts`): 23 Polish cities, biggest first,
  then 15 major European cities. Each has a stable id (`pl-krakow`), name, country, centre and a
  rough radius. Search is accent-insensitive ("krakow" → Kraków, "lodz" → Łódź).
- **Stored per user** in `ter_home_city` (migration 7, one row) as the catalogue id only, with a
  `HomeCityChosen` event (city id, no coordinates) appended to the outbox in the same transaction.
  The event's delivery bumps `dataVersion`, so every screen refreshes.
- **Camera rules:**
  - Turf Map: opens on the Home City's bounds (centre ± radius, so the whole city fits any screen)
    and flies to a newly chosen city. Chip "⌂ <city>" recentres (long press: change city).
  - No Home City: today's behaviour (fit all tracks once, else follow the user) plus a "⌂ Your city"
    chip that opens the picker.
  - Record map: starts on the Home City until the first GPS fix, then follows the user. A city
    chosen from its chip is flown to (following stops; Recenter resumes it).
  - Activity detail: unchanged (the activity's own bounds).
- **Where to pick it:** Me menu → City, the Record tab's chip at the top (before a recording starts;
  founder's request, 2026-10-19), or the Turf Map chip. Not part of sign-in or onboarding.

## Why

- **Privacy:** a home city is coarse location and may hint where someone lives (risk R8 in
  `docs/poc-plan.md`). A bundled catalogue means no search query or location leaves the phone, and
  only an id is stored, in the user's own database. Never logged, never in diagnostics.
- **Ships over the air:** JS and a SQLite migration only; no native module, no permission, no
  `app.json` change. The runtime fingerprint is unchanged.
- **Works offline and on first launch**, before any location permission: today an empty Turf Map
  without a fix opens on MapLibre's default (0,0, open sea).

Rejected for now: online geocoding (Nominatim/Photon: network, privacy review, rate limits);
"nearest city from my location" (needs the location permission outside recording; easy follow-up
with `distanceM` over the catalogue); a free map pin as Home (vaguer to show, more personal);
storing the city in Identity (it is about turf and maps, not about who the user is).

## Consequences

- Catalogue ids live in user databases: never rename or reuse one. Removing a city is safe (the id
  reads as no Home City). Adding cities is a JS-only change.
- Future "city coverage" (open since v0.1.0) can use the Home City as its denominator's area.
- Tests: domain (`city.test.ts`: search, folding, bounds, catalogue integrity), use cases with the
  real SQLite repository (`home-city.test.ts`), migration count 7, Maestro `12-home-city` (CP-10).
